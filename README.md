Robust Multi-Condition Road Damage Analysis for Tropical Urban Environments

This repository contains the research and implementation work for our final-year undergraduate research project on road damage detection and analysis.

Research Focus

The project aims to develop a robust computer vision system for detecting and analysing road damage under challenging real-world conditions, with particular focus on tropical urban road environments.

The research consists of multiple components covering areas such as:

Road damage detection and classification

Road damage segmentation

Domain adaptation and cross-domain generalization

Robustness under varying environmental and road conditions
